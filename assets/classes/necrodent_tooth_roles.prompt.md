# Backenzähne und Wachzähne

Mit dem eingebauten ImageGen auf transparentem Hintergrund erzeugt. Die Milchzahn-Sprites waren die Stilreferenz.

Original sprites for Denti Divine Dentistry. A transparent PNG 4 columns by 2 rows contact sheet. Exactly eight small game sprites. Each row the SAME creature in four ordered animation poses at equal scale, almost front facing. Row 1 is the undead Backenzahn, squat wide, rounded rectangular enamel crown with jagged broken upper corner, funny dark aubergine wide mandible, two bright gold pinpricks in dark sockets, three thick root feet, subtle dull gold edges at the crown. Row 2 is the undead Wachzahn, upright dark dusty slate porcelain tooth with two root feet and a broad shield-shaped ivory enamel plate naturally part of its crown, jade eyes in dark cavities and a jade center chip. No metal shield item or weapon, shape is a tooth. For both rows left-to-right poses are standing with closed mouth, lifted root walking step, open-mouthed bite, lying on the ground sideways with closed or dim eyes. Reference is the original milk tooth sprite sheet, match its glossy painted 2D enamel, dark warm outline, expressive monster personality and clear proportions while giving these two their distinct silhouettes. Crisp contour and minimal details for 30–40 pixels on screen. Rigid conceptual grid, equal gaps, generous transparent gutters, no overlapping sprites, no text, no grid lines, no backgrounds, no smoke or blank cells.

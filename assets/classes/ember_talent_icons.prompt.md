# ember · Talent-Atlas

Eingebautes ImageGen mit `transparent_background: true`. Pro Ast ein Quellbogen.
Der verlustfreie WebP-Atlas hat 8 × 3 Zellen zu 192 × 192 Pixeln.

```text
Create a NEW painted 2D video game sprite sheet for Denti: Divine Dentistry. The supplied talent atlases (assets/classes/talent_icons.webp, molar_talent_icons.webp) are a style reference ONLY. Match their warm ivory enamel, glossy gold and painted metal, saturated accent colors, warm directional highlights, slight brush texture, bold dark-brown outlines and chunky, toy-like three-dimensional shapes. Make exactly 7 symbols on a strict evenly spaced layout of FOUR square columns and TWO square rows, reading left to right, top to bottom; unused cells completely empty. Keep 25% clear space within each cell and every silhouette well inside its cell. Each object is large and unmistakable even at 28 pixels. Real clean transparent alpha background: zero alpha in gutters, borders and empty cells, no cast shadows, haze, backdrop glows, base plates, text, numbers, grid, borders or watermarks.
Branch: Esse. Palette: orange, rust and charcoal. Use a wide 2:1 canvas.
Top row, column 1: small bundle of tinder with its first tiny single golden flame.
Top row, column 2: small copper oil can, pouring a splash of flame from the spout.
Top row, column 3: luminous spherical molten lava core suspended inside a chunky rough charcoal stone ring.
Top row, column 4: solid piece of smoldering coal with a warm reddish crack.
Bottom row, column 1: chunky open forge mouth ejecting a distinct shower of yellow sparks.
Bottom row, column 2: dark steel anvil with a bright dancing flame directly above it.
Bottom row, column 3: circular ring of fire confined low to the ground, visible elliptic opening.
Bottom row, column 4: completely transparent empty space.
```

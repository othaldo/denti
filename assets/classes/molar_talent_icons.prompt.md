# Neue Talent-Icons für Good Old Molar

Generiert mit dem eingebauten ImageGen-Tool mit `transparent_background: true`.
Der gemeinsame Stilbezug war der bestehende `talent_icons.webp`-Atlas.
Die Motivliste stammt aus `docs/TALENT_TREE_V3_PLAN.md`.

Der verlustfreie WebP-Atlas enthält 5 × 3 Zellen à 192 × 192 Pixel.
Die Zeilen entsprechen Werkzeugkasten, Fachpraxis und Standfest.

## Gemeinsamer Prompt

```text
Create a NEW painted 2D video game sprite sheet for Denti: Divine Dentistry. The supplied original talent atlas is a style reference ONLY. Match its warm ivory enamel, glossy gold and painted metal, saturated accent colors, warm directional highlights, slight brush texture, bold dark-brown outlines and chunky, toy-like three-dimensional shapes. Make exactly FIVE symbols on a strict evenly spaced layout of THREE square columns and TWO square rows, with the lower right cell completely empty. Use 25% generous clear space within each cell and keep each silhouette, its sparks, and its trails well inside its cell. Each object is large and unmistakable even at 28 pixels. No art overlaps adjacent cells. Real clean transparent alpha background throughout outside the shapes: zero alpha on every gutter, border and the empty cell, no cast shadows, grey haze, backdrop glows, base plates, text, numbers, grid, borders or watermarks. Nonsymbol detail is subordinate to the central silhouette.
```

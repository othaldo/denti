# rinser · Talent-Atlas

Eingebautes ImageGen mit `transparent_background: true`. Pro Ast ein Quellbogen.
Der verlustfreie WebP-Atlas hat 8 × 3 Zellen zu 192 × 192 Pixeln.

## Leitung

Referenz für die Generierung: die freigestellte Zeile Spülung. Verwendete Quelle: `exec-1cb1e6db-5f5f-4560-bad9-3170f96aa93e.png`.

```text
Generate a new transparent game icon atlas. The attached transparent sheet is a rendering reference only: it shows the expected strong outlines, cartoon painted enamel and metal, and real crisp cutout edges. Do not copy its subject matter. Deliver seven NEW small-scale illustrated symbols in the same true cutout style for the Spültechniker class in Denti: Divine Dentistry. IMPORTANT the canvas is alpha transparent, all blank area and gutters are truly transparent, no white or gray blur, tile backgrounds, dropped shadows, or scenery. Make a broad 2:1 layout, four square cells in the top row and four square cells in the bottom row with consistent separation and generous open gutters. Top row, from left: copper cable with a bright spark at the stripped tip; a golden lightning bolt splitting into three prongs; an electric yellow bolt centered inside a complete thick aqua water ring; a triangular golden voltage warning plaque with a black lightning pictogram, no text. Bottom row, from left: rounded aqua drop with two little golden sparks; copper grounding stake alongside a little silver shield; chunky dark-blue pressurized spray nozzle with a strong narrow white-aqua jet; last cell entirely clear. No text or numbers. Keep the icon footprints isolated even when reduced to 28 pixels. The uninterrupted space between the symbols and around every edge has NO art and zero alpha.
```

Keep 25% clear space within each cell and every silhouette well inside its cell. Each object is large and unmistakable even at 28 pixels. Real clean transparent alpha background: zero alpha in gutters, borders and empty cells, no cast shadows, haze, backdrop glows, base plates, text, numbers, grid, borders or watermarks.

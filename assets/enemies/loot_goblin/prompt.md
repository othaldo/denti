## Integration

The generated layout is not a perfect mathematical grid. `LootGoblinRig.REGIONS` selects the actual complete shapes. Mipmaps are built once during menu warmup. Native captures in `docs/screenshots/loot_goblin/` verify transparency, joint placement and combat-size readability. Audio cues are short synthesized chimes, authored separately from the image generation.
